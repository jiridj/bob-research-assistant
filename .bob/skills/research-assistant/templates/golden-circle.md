## Guidelines for Using This Template

**Keep It Concise:**
- Target 2-3 pages total
- Use clear, simple language
- Avoid jargon and buzzwords
- Every sentence should add value

**Make It Relatable:**
- Use stories and examples throughout
- Show, don't just tell
- Use concrete scenarios over abstract concepts
- Include real customer/user voices when possible

**Start with WHY:**
- Spend the most time on WHY—it's your foundation
- Make sure WHY is inspiring and authentic
- Test if your WHY resonates emotionally
- Ensure HOW and WHAT naturally flow from WHY

**Remember:**
People don't buy WHAT you do, they buy WHY you do it. The goal is to inspire belief, not just describe features.
